Colombia - published guidance for the officials who decide migration cases
Checked 2026-11-15. Re-run: python3 download_co.py (from the folder above this one).

Result in one paragraph
Colombia publishes a substantial amount of real officer guidance, but only for one of
its two authorities. Migración Colombia (UAEMC: border control, foreigners' registration
and permits, enforcement/sanctions) publishes the working documents of its quality
system for its three case-handling processes: a process manual, flow-chart procedures,
and about 35 current guides (guías) and instructions (instructivos) written for migration
officers - e.g. MCG.01 general instructions for border control (107 pages), MCG.03
treatment of inadmitted/deported/expelled persons, MCG.04 control of children, MEG.02
handling of extranjería applications, MEG.12 Temporary Protection Permit, MVG.12 the
sanctions/deportation procedure, MVG.18 verification visits. These are the target
material and all current versions were saved. The Foreign Ministry (Cancillería: visas,
refugee status through CONARE, statelessness) publishes no officer guidance: only
resolutions, and applicant information. Its internal visa and refugee procedures exist
(codes DP-PT-150, DP-PT-168, DP-PT-173 are cited in a 2020 audit report) but sit in an
internal "Sistema Maestro". Its website also refuses scripts (see below).

What was saved (90 documents, 306 MB; 167 MB of that are two Excel files)
  kind "guidance": 66
    - 55 current quality-system documents of Migración Colombia (sig/): 1 process manual,
      3 process characterisations, 10 procedures (xlsx flow charts), 27 guides and
      annexes, 9 instructions, 2 x 2 "normograma" lists of applicable norms (PDF list
      and an Excel workbook in which the cited acts are embedded as objects; the two
      workbooks are 121 MB and 46 MB and were kept as published, not unpacked), plus
      "Otras normas de Extranjería".
    - 1 older guide still on the server but no longer linked (guias/: MVG.14 v3, human
      rights and transitory migration rooms).
    - 5 directivas (2016-2019) that adopt process documents; one is present twice
      (listing page and package).
    - 2 circulares (2020) and 3 legal opinions (conceptos) of the legal office.
  kind "procedural act": 24 resolutions, 2013-2026 (three copies of Resolución 0971 de
    2021 among them, from three sources): Migración Colombia resolutions on entry/stay/
    exit (2061 de 2020, 2231 de 2021), the extranjería manuals (2201 de 2013, 1499 de
    2018), identity cards, the Temporary Protection Statute for Venezuelans and its
    certificates, sanctions (0360 de 2015, 2945 de 2020), and the Foreign Ministry's visa
    resolution 5477 de 2022 with 12509 de 2024 and the PEP permit resolutions.
  Dates: for acts, the date or year of the act. For quality-system documents there is no
  stated approval date; the date column and file prefix carry the date stored in the
  file itself (PDF creation date / Excel last-modified), which is close to, but not
  exactly, the approval date of that version (2020-05 to 2026-09). The version number
  is in the file name.

Sources used
- https://www.migracioncolombia.gov.co/planeacion-y-estadistica/procesos-y-procedimientos
  ZIP packages per process. Taken: Gestión Control Migratorio, Gestión Verificación
  Migratoria, Gestión Extranjería (all categories). Members are saved byte for byte;
  the ZIPs are not kept (listings/zip-manifest.tsv records which member came from
  which ZIP). Left out on purpose and listed in not-obtained.tsv: information-security
  and records-management documents inside the extranjería package, Decreto 216 de 2021.
  Not taken: the 13 support processes (strategy, IT, HR, citizen service, contracting,
  finance, legal, records, discipline, audit...). The citizen-service package holds
  service-desk guides (ERCG.04 Guía de Atención al Ciudadano, ERCG.14 appointments);
  the legal package holds AGJG.04 (answering consultations) - not case-decision guidance.
- https://www.migracioncolombia.gov.co/normativa-y-documentacion/normativa/ (circulares,
  directivas, resoluciones, conceptos): 4 + 7 + 34 + 2 items listed; only those about
  migration case handling were kept. The site now lists very few circulares and
  directivas; most of the entity's directivas since 2012 are not on it.
- https://nexusmigracion.cancilleria.gov.co/marcos-normativos/marco-normativo-nacional
  (Foreign Ministry migration portal): 9 resolutions kept; laws, decrees (incl. Decreto
  1067 de 2015 and Decreto 089 de 2025 on refugee matters), CONPES papers not taken.
- robots.txt: www.migracioncolombia.gov.co has none (404); nexusmigracion allows the
  paths used.

Refugee status (CONARE)
No published guidance for decision-makers was found. The procedure is in Decreto 1067
de 2015 (Title 3) as amended (a decree, not downloaded); the Cancillería publishes an
applicant guide ("Guía Refugio en Colombia", 2023/2024) - applicant information, and on
the blocked host. Migración Colombia's side of asylum at the border is covered in
MCG.03 (section 5.6 "Refugio o asilo").

Completeness doubts
- Only the current version of each guide is published; earlier versions are not.
- The packages sit in a folder named after a month ("SEPTIEMBRE"); they are replaced
  periodically. The script finds them through the listing page, so a re-run after an
  update adds the new versions next to the old ones.
- Whether the embedded acts inside the two normograma workbooks include directivas not
  otherwise published was not examined.
